New application — app-translations-upload COT 1COT 3IDP 1IDP 3

  • Delivered a net-new Next.js tool that integrates AI-generated translations end-to-end: upload a spreadsheet → resolve entry UIDs → upsert entries in Contentstack → publish across every supported locale, all in one click.
  • Before: a content editor had to log into Contentstack and hand-enter each localized string, one entry per locale at a time — a full translation batch could burn most of a working day per person.
  • After: AI-produced translations flow from spreadsheet to published entries in minutes, self-serve, with no engineer or editor in the manual loop.
  • Removes engineering and manual content-entry from the critical path for every future localization drop, freeing team capacity for feature work.
